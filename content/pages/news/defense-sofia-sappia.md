On Monday, November 16th at 14:30, [member/sofia-sappia] successfully defended her PhD thesis titled 'AI-powered pregnancy risk detection in low-resource settings'.

Every day, around 700 women die from preventable pregnancy-related complications, most of them in low- and middle-income countries where access to ultrasound and trained sonographers remains limited. This research advanced BabyChecker, an AI-enabled system that helps novice users acquire standardized ultrasound scans with a low-cost device and automatically flags high-risk pregnancies. The system supports frontline healthcare workers with no prior ultrasound experience in identifying twin pregnancies and babies in risky positions. Through the ACOUSLIC-AI challenge, AI developers worldwide competed to build an algorithm that automatically measures fetal abdominal circumference on scans acquired by novices. A simple addition to the standard scanning protocol, tilting the ultrasound probe, improved visualization of the lower uterus, where the fetal head is often captured and where key information on placenta position can be found. 

We congratulate Sofía and wish her good luck in her position as data engineer at ScreenPoint Medical!
