On Friday, November 13th at 10:30, [member/joeran-bosma] successfully defended his PhD thesis titled 'Automated Data Mining for Medical Image Analysis'.

Artificial intelligence (AI) detects prostate cancer on MRI at expert level. In the international PI-CAI study using previously acquired scans, the best AI system outperformed the average of 62 radiologists. It produced about half as many false alarms while detecting the same proportion of clinically significant cancers. 

This thesis also shows that existing medical reports reduce the manual work needed to develop AI. They enabled training on 17,896 MRI examinations from 24 hospitals in ten countries. Adding automatically labelled data improved prostate cancer detection. 

Two newly developed AI tournaments provide frameworks for fair comparisons of AI that processes medical texts and images. Together, these findings bring reliable support for doctors closer to practice. Studies in routine care must establish how best to use AI to benefit patients.
