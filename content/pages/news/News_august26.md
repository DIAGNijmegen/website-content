## DIAG at MICCAI 2026

DIAG is getting ready for [MICCAI 2026](https://conferences.miccai.org/2026/en/default.asp)!" Our members are involved in a wide range of workshops and challenge organisations, oral presentations, and poster sessions! Below, you can find an overview of where to meet DIAG members at MICCAI this year.

One event we would particularly like to highlight the [COMPAYL++](https://www.compayl.com) on September 27th. This full-day event brings together three MICCAI satellite events focused on computational pathology including the [COMPAYL’26 workshop](https://www.compayl.com/workshop), [the REG2 challenge](https://reg2026.grand-challenge.org/), and a tutorial, featuring a keynote by [member/Nadieh-Khalili].

On October 1, the Thoracic Image Analysis Workshop will bring together researchers working on a wide range of thoracic imaging applications. The workshop is co-organized by [member/colin-jacobs].

As always, DIAG members are (co-)organising challenges at MICCAI:   
The Breast Imaging Group MRI Challenge, co-organized by [member/henkjan-huisman], will take place on September 27. The challenge focuses on predicting breast cancer risk in women.   
On October 1, Learn2Reg, co-organized by [member/alessa-hering], will take place. This registration challenge introduces two tasks involving the registration of PET/CT images and inspiratory and expiratory chest CT scans.  
The CHIMERA-Agent challenge will also take place on October 1. The challenge aims to develop a flexible AI agent for realistic clinical decision-making in prostate cancer biopsy.

DIAG members will also contribute to several presentations and programme activities at MICCAI. On September 30, [member/sarah-de-boer] and [member/Hartmut-haentze] will give oral presentations as part of the [Open Data Track](OPEN DATA) on the 30th of September (which is co-organized by [member/alessa-hering]). 

We are excited for MICCAI 2026 and look forward to seeing many of you in Strasbourg!

## DIAG at ECP

DIAG members are orginazing the Computational Pathology Symposium at ECP in Stockholm this year. On the 13th of September [members/Jeroen-van-der-laak] and [members/frederique-meeuwsen] are organising an exciting event focused on computational pathology.
