## Background
 Most vulvar squamous cell carcinomas (VSCC) are HPV-independent and develop from lichen sclerosus (LS) or differentiated vulvar intraepithelial neoplasia (dVIN). While only a subset of these lesions progress to cancer, current histopathology cannot reliably identify which patients are at highest risk, potentially leading to overtreatment.

## Aim
Can AI applied to histopathology slides predict progression from LS/dVIN to VSCC?
