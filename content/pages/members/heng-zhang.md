Heng Zhang was a PhD candidate at the Department of Pathology at Radboudumc and DIAG/CPG, working on improving the understanding and treatment of colorectal mucinous adenocarcinoma by researching deeper into chromosomal copy number aberrations and chromosomal copy number heterogeneity to discover the relationship between these factors and the prognosis of stage II colorectal mucinous adenocarcinoma.
