Jos Thannhauser (1989) is an Assistant Professor within the departments of Cardiology and Medical Imaging of the Radboudumc. He studied Technical Medicine at the University of Twente. After graduating his master's degree in 2015, he worked at the Department of Cardiology of the Radboudumc, where he performed clinical research and managed several clinical trials in the field of cardiac rhythm disorders and devices. In 2021, he finished his PhD entitled "Technical Innovations in Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest: On the Road to a Smart Defibrillator". After his PhD, he was involved in multiple research projects as a postdoctoral researcher (Radboudumc, University of Twente, Radboud University). His main areas of interest are intravascular imaging, artificial intelligence, and cardiovascular (electro)physiology.

His research group, part of the [Diagnostic Image Analysis Group](http://www.diagnijmegen.nl), focuses on artificial intelligence based solutions in the field of cardiology. Since 2023, Jos is manager of the Cardiology lab Radboudumc and Amsterdam UMC ([CARA Lab](https://www.cara-ai-lab.nl/)), a research collaboration between academic and industrial partners. The lab builds robust, trustworthy AI for real-time analysis of intracoronary optical coherence tomography (OCT) during cardiac catheterization. Its aim is to bring automated image interpretation directly into the cath lab to support clinical decisions. In 2026 the lab marked its first PhD defense and introduced OCT-AID-lite, a lightweight model for near-real-time plaque segmentation. The CARA lab is part of the [Innovation Center for Artificial Intelligence (ICAI)](https://www.icai.ai/).
