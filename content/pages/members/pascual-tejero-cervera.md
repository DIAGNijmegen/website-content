Pascual Tejero Cervera has an academic background in Biomedical Engineering from the Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV) in Spain. He holds a Master's Degree in Biomedical Computing from the Technical University of Munich (TUM) in Germany, specialising in deep learning methods and machine learning algorithms for medical image analysis.

Since June 2026, he has been a PhD candidate at the Diagnostic Image Analysis Group (DIAG) at Radboudumc, and also collaborates with the health-tech company [Plain Medical](https://plain-medical.com/). His research is part of the Next Generation RECIST project, focusing on the development of AI methods for lesion detection, segmentation, and longitudinal analysis with the aim of improving the consistency and efficiency of RECIST-based assessments. His PhD supervisors are [member/alessa-hering] and [member/bram-van-ginneken].
