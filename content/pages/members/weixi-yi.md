Weixi Yi is a PhD candidate at the [UCL Hawkes Institute](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/hawkes-institute), working with [Prof Yipeng Hu](https://profiles.ucl.ac.uk/5178-yipeng-hu) and [Dr Shaheer U. Saeed](https://www.sems.qmul.ac.uk/staff/s.saeed/). He is also a visiting researcher at Radboud University Medical Center, working in the [Diagnostic Image Analysis Group (DIAG)](https://www.diagnijmegen.nl/). His research interests include prostate cancer diagnosis using T2-Only MRI, medical foundation models, and agentic AI systems. He received his master’s degree in Medical Robotics and Artificial Intelligence from UCL and his bachelor’s degree in Communication Engineering from Shandong University, China.
