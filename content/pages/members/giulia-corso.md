Giulia Corso is a guest researcher at the Computational Pathology Group, where she works under the supervision of [member/jeroen-van-der-laak]. She joined the group on 1 November 2026 for an eight-month research project. Her work focuses on prompt sensitivity of pathology vision-language models and weakly supervised learning on whole-slide images for clinical prediction tasks.
 
Giulia is currently a third-year PhD student in the Italian National PhD Program in Artificial Intelligence for Health and Life Sciences, hosted by University Campus Bio-Medico of Rome and carried out at the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, where she also obtained her bachelor's and master's degrees in Computer Engineering. In collaboration with IRCCS Humanitas Research Hospital, her research focuses on developing AI methods for computational pathology under real-world clinical conditions.
