Diletta Fioretti is a Master's student in Biomedical Engineering at the Politecnico of Turin, specializing in Digital Healthcare and Artificial Intelligence. Starting September 2026, she will conduct her thesis research in collaboration with the Computational Pathology Group at Radboudumc under the supervision of [member/salma-dammak] and [member/geert-litjens]. Her research project focuses on the automatic detection of liver hypertrophy in drug safety studies, developing a comparative multi-modal AI model that combines digital histology slides with blood markers, with the aim of reducing the time and workload of drug development while lowering the rate of missed hypertrophy.
