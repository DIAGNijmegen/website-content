Thomas van den Heuvel was a postdoc in the [Diagnostic Image Analysis Group](http://www.diagnijmegen.nl). His work was part of the [POCUS-AI]( https://www.diagnijmegen.nl/research/pocus-ai/) research line in which he combined point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) with artificial intelligence. This research line was conducted in close collaboration with the [Medical Ultrasound Imaging Centre](http://music.radboudimaging.nl/index.php/Home). His main focus was development of software for automated detection of maternal risk factors in resource-limited settings. He was supervised by [member/bram-van-ginneken].

Thomas studied Biomedical Engineering at [Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)](https://www.tue.nl/). During his studies he participated in the [Honors Program of the TU/e]( https://studiegids.tue.nl/opleidingen/tue-honors-academy/?L=0) and did an internship at [Yale University](https://www.yale.edu/). In January 2019, he obtained his PhD degree from the [Radboud University](https://www.ru.nl/) in Nijmegen, the Netherlands.
