Julia Haentjes was a master student at DIAG. She was a master student in Biomedical Engineering at Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e). In April 2026, she started her internship under the supervision of [Bogdan Obreja](https://www.diagnijmegen.nl/people/bogdan-obreja/). In September 2016, she finished her internship.

The goal of her project was to improve an existing deep learning-based lung nodule detection algorithm that was previously developed and trained on non-screening CT data. In the project, she adapted and retrained the current model by using screening CT data and evaluated the performance of the model with screening data.
