Yannick Kirchhoff is a PhD student at the [Division of Medical Image Computing](https://www.dkfz.de/en/medical-image-computing/) at the [German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ)](https://www.dkfz.de/en/) and a member of the [Helmholtz Information and Data Science School for Health (HIDSS4Health)](https://www.hidss4health.de/). He is also a visiting researcher at Radboud University Medical Center, working in the [Diagnostic Image Analysis Group (DIAG)](https://www.diagnijmegen.nl/). His research focuses on general method development for deep learning in 3D medical imaging, with a particular interest in foundation models for radiology. He also works on longitudinal imaging, developing methods that use prior exams as context, from segmenting lesions consistently across follow-up scans to tracking them interactively. Most of his research code is open source and available on [GitHub](https://github.com/ykirchhoff).
Before his PhD, he received his BSc and MSc degrees in physics from [Heidelberg University](https://www.uni-heidelberg.de/en), spending a year of his Master's at the [Institute for Particle Physics Phenomenology (IPPP)](https://www.ippp.dur.ac.uk/) in Durham.
